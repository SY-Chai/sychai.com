The 2021 mission mainly involved collecting and depositing randomly arranged cars at respective locations while avoiding obstacles like barriers. I learnt various sorting and searching algorithms to optimise the collection of cars and implemented graph traversal algorithms to determine the shortest path to take.

The 2022 mission involved crossing obstacles as well as detecting various types of objects. To do so, the robot base was redesigned to be optimal for crossing, and we improved the sensing algorithm to allow the robot to continue moving while sensing, saving time.
